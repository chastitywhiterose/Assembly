About the Cover

The reason I wanted Alan Turing on the cover for Assembly Arithmetic Algorithms was that he represents 3 significant things the world tends to forget.

First, he was a hero who probably shortened World War 2 a lot by building a machine to crack the Enigma Machine. He probably saved more lives than we will ever know, considering how the German Nazis may have won the war without his work.

 Second, his mathematical discoveries and writings formed the foundation for modern computers and programming languages. His Turing Machine and concept of Turing Completeness, based on the tests he described, helped make my book a reality. I appreciate him as a mathematician, aside from his heroic efforts in the war.

Third, despite his achievements, he was treated as a criminal because he was homosexual and was tortured, forced to undergo chemical castration, and driven to suicide by the British government. Ironically, he was given the same treatment by his own government as the Jews and Homosexuals were by Adolph Hitler and the German Nazis.

As a Transgender person living in a time of great persecution by the United States Government against my people, Alan Turing is a symbol to remind others not to make the same mistake against me and others of my kind as was done to Alan. We cannot repay him for all the good work he did to make our digital world possible, nor can we give him back his life, which was taken from him far too soon. I hope to meet him in the next life and show him what I have learned about arithmetic because of him.

I dedicate this book to Alan Turing and the many talented people, both living and dead, who are denied basic human rights and the chance to live to their full potential and use their gifts.
